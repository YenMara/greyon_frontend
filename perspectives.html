    The world as we see it

    We’re all individuals. Each formed by a collection of personal experiences. Every one with a library of stories to share, and like the cities where you will find us, no two are alike.

    Within The Peninsula we celebrate this diversity of character. It’s a vital part of our culture, and a distinction which inspires and influences every single guest experience.

    Through Peninsula Perspectives, we’d like to welcome you to our destination cities, which we are passionate about sharing. We’ll also introduce a few of the people who make the visits to our hotels so special. You’ll learn about some of the events that have shaped their lives, hear their thoughts about the cities they call home – and by getting to know them, you’ll get to know us better.

    We invite you to come explore and see the world from our perspective.
